# Features - Phase 1

_Created 2026-05-10_

## Overview

General feature list for an Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system.

## Patient Management

- Patient registration and demographics
- Unique patient identifier (MRN)
- Contact information and emergency contacts
- Patient consent and privacy preferences
- Patient search and lookup
- Merge duplicate patient records

## Clinical Documentation

- Progress notes and SOAP notes
- Encounter/visit documentation
- Problem list management
- Allergies and adverse reactions
- Medication list and reconciliation
- Immunization records
- Medical, surgical, family, and social history
- Vital signs tracking
- Templates for common note types

## Orders and Results

- Computerized provider order entry (CPOE)
- Lab order entry and result viewing
- Imaging/radiology orders and results
- Referrals and consultation requests
- Order tracking and status updates
- Result review and acknowledgment

## Prescriptions / e-Prescribing

- Electronic prescription creation
- Drug interaction and allergy checking
- Formulary lookup
- Prescription renewal and refills
- Controlled substance prescribing (EPCS)
- Medication history

## Diagnosis and Coding

- ICD-10-CM diagnosis coding
- Code search and validation
- Problem-to-diagnosis linking

## Scheduling

- Appointment scheduling and calendar views
- Provider and resource availability
- Recurring appointments
- Appointment reminders (SMS/email)
- Waitlist management
- Cancellation and no-show tracking

## Billing and Revenue Cycle

- Charge capture
- Payment posting
- Patient statements and invoicing
- Accounts receivable tracking

## Lab and Diagnostic Integration

- HL7 / FHIR interface for lab results
- Imaging system (PACS/DICOM) integration
- External lab vendor connectivity
- Result trending and graphing

## Reporting and Analytics

- Clinical quality measures
- Patient panel reports
- Operational dashboards
- Custom report builder
- Population health metrics
- Regulatory reporting (MIPS, etc.)

## Patient Portal

- Secure patient login
- Appointment requests and viewing
- Access to lab results and visit summaries
- Secure messaging with care team
- Prescription refill requests
- Bill pay
- Telehealth/video visits

## Security and Compliance

- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- User authentication and MFA
- Audit logs of access and changes
- HIPAA compliance
- Data encryption (in transit and at rest)
- Break-the-glass emergency access
- Session timeout and lockout

## Interoperability

- HL7 v2 messaging
- FHIR API support
- C-CDA document exchange
- Direct secure messaging
- Health information exchange (HIE) connectivity

## Administration

- Provider and staff profiles
- Facility/location management
- Master data (drugs, labs, codes) maintenance
- System configuration and preferences
- License and credential tracking

## Notifications and Alerts

- Clinical decision support alerts
- Drug-drug and drug-allergy alerts
- Preventive care reminders
- Critical lab value notifications
- Task and inbox management

## PACS (Simple Imaging Reader)

- X-ray image upload and viewing
- Ultrasound image and clip viewing
- DICOM file support
- Basic viewer tools (zoom, pan, rotate, brightness/contrast, measure)
- Study and series navigation
- Link images to patient record and encounter
- Radiologist/clinician reporting and sign-off
- Image download and sharing

## Telemedicine Platform

- Video consultation (one-on-one and group)
- Audio-only consultations
- Virtual waiting room
- In-call screen sharing
- In-call chat and file sharing
- Appointment-linked video sessions
- Recording and consent capture
- e-Prescribing during virtual visit
- Post-visit summary and follow-up

## Communication and Integrations

- Email integration (transactional and clinical messaging)
- WhatsApp integration (appointment reminders, notifications, two-way messaging)
- Internal chat system (staff-to-staff messaging)
- Group/team channels
- Direct messages between providers and staff
- File and image sharing within chat
- Patient-context messaging (link chat to patient record)
- Read receipts and presence indicators
- Notification routing across channels (email, WhatsApp, in-app)
